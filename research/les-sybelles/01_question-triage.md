# Les Sybelles Level 3 — atomic-question triage

**Date:** 21 October 2026  
**Method:** Apply the 173-question core intelligence framework and the Portes du Soleil domain-level question method. The atomic bank is an internal coverage and audit tool, not public FAQ copy.

## Page job

Explain the physical ski network sold under the Les Sybelles name, distinguish the linked core from areas that are not currently connected by maintained ski links, and help readers judge what the headline size means for a week on snow. The page should support decisions about base, realistic cross-domain days, pass choice, ability, snow-height trade-offs and operational checks.

## CORE — must resolve before publication

1. What exactly does the operator include in Les Sybelles for winter 2026–27?
2. Which resorts/sectors are connected continuously by lifts and pistes?
3. Is Saint-Colomban-des-Villards linked to the main network in 2026–27, or is its former connection discontinued?
4. If Saint-Colomban is disconnected, does the current full-domain pass still cover its local lifts? What does the ticket contract say?
5. What is the operator's current definition and source for 310 km of piste?
6. What are the current official run and lift counts, and do those totals include disconnected local areas?
7. What are the lowest and highest lift-served points of the connected core, distinguished from village elevations and summits?
8. Which lifts and piste corridors join Le Corbier, La Toussuire, Saint-Jean-d'Arves, Saint-Sorlin-d'Arves and Les Bottières?
9. Which links depend on a single lift, and what alternative exists if it closes?
10. Which sectors are central to cross-domain movement, and which sit at the edge?
11. Can the main cross-domain routes be completed by green/blue pistes, or do they require red runs?
12. Where are the beginner zones and what sector passes let beginners avoid buying the full domain pass?
13. What share of the mapped pistes is easy/intermediate/difficult, using a consistent source and definition?
14. What does the pass cost for six days in 2026–27, for which age band and purchase channel, and how does that compare with local-sector passes?
15. What are the opening dates by sector, and which dates are published plans rather than guaranteed operation?
16. How do the high Ouillon/Les 3 Lacs sectors compare with lower village returns for snow retention and spring conditions?
17. Is there a reproducible snow-history series by elevation, and how does it compare with observed snow depth?
18. How do the six named resorts differ in base elevation, settlement form and direct access to the linked network?
19. Which transport links connect the Maurienne rail stations and motorway exits to each base, and what is season-specific?
20. Which claims are safe to publish now, and which need operator confirmation?

## SUPPORT — answer where evidence justifies it

- Terrain character and slope aspect by sector.
- Drag-lift share and implications for snowboarders.
- Beginner lift/pass products by sector.
- Family facilities at the domain level, without duplicating resort pages.
- Snowpark, boardercross and other fun zones.
- Typical access from Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne and Saint-Avre–La Chambre.
- Six-day adult pass comparison with other large French domains, with same-season and same-age-band caveats.
- Domain history and development of the connected network.
- A small number of route examples, but only after route dependencies and lift rides are checked.
- Mountain food and après patterns only if they add domain-level value; named venues belong on resort pages.

## INTERNAL / DEFERRED

- Individual restaurant menus, accommodation listings, lesson prices and childcare details: keep on Level 4/5 pages.
- Current snow depths, open lifts and weather: link to official live sources rather than hard-code into evergreen copy.
- Unverified claims about crowding, “quiet slopes”, affordability or family-friendliness: do not repeat as facts without comparable evidence.
- Exact travel times from every airport, train station or base: only include if a current route or timetable is recorded.
- Detailed avalanche, off-piste and safety guidance: link to the relevant official bulletin and local advice; do not infer safety from a piste map.
- Any “one of France's largest” ranking without a defined, comparable measurement basis.

## Evidence and publication rules

- One question may support multiple claims; one claim may answer several questions. Keep claim IDs stable.
- Distinguish operator-reported totals, independent measurements, map-derived calculations, modelled climate data and interpretation.
- A shared brand or pass does not prove a continuous lift-and-piste connection.
- No route matrix, critical-lift ranking or exact lift-ride count until the underlying graph is reproducible and checked against the official map.
- Unresolved high-risk topology and pass-coverage questions block publication. Niche questions can be excluded if the exclusion is documented.
