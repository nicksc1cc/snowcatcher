## les-portes-du-soleil/a-human-story/
- 1956: first international lift link, Super-Châtel to Morgins (Pierre Béteille).
- 1964: model shown at the National Exhibition in Lausanne. 1965: name chosen (from the pass at Les Crosets).
- 1968: Champéry to Avoriaz link via Chavanette; route to the top of the Swiss Wall.

- Current season 28.11.2026 to 18.04.2027; general season mid December to late April; opening times 08:30 to 17:00.
- New for 2026/27: Pointe du Chéry, 6 seat detachable chairlift (Les Gets, Mont Chéry).
- Interconnection: "The ski resorts Abondance, La Chapelle d'Abondance, and St Jean d'Aulps are not directly connected to the slopes of Les Portes du Soleil, but can only be reached by ski bus."
- Valley runs lead to almost all base stations; exceptions Super Morzine (Morzine) and Panthiaz (La Chapelle d'Abondance).
- Longest run Chamossière to Morzine, 11 km, 1,002 m drop. Most difficult run Chavanette (Champéry), 1 km, 330 m drop, gradients up to 100%.
- Lifts to cross from one end to the other: 15 one way, 14 the other.
- Lift commentary: most slopes served by fast 4 and 6 seat chairlifts; one covered (bubble) lift only, in Champéry; older lifts still common in Morgins, Champoussin and Torgon.
- Snow reliability: upper slopes very snow sure; "All important connecting slopes ... are covered with artificial snow"; valley run practically always open.
- Test ratings: size 5 stars; beginners 4 stars; snow reliability text above. "Most, but not all, resorts are connected by easy slopes."
